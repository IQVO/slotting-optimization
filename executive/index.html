warehouse-systems · executive briefing · October 2026slotting-optimization
Owns the forward-slotting decision the rest of the fleet leaves implicit: today fast movers can sit in far slots while slow movers hold the best ones. It computes a reviewable SlotPlan proposal per site and demand window from event-fed local copies (demand, product facts, slot facts, pack levels), a planner either accepts or rejects it, and only an Approved plan changes the site's forward slot map. It plans but never executes: approved moves leave as CloudEvents and are executed on the floor by wes-work-planning / fulfillment-execution / inventory-storage, with StockRelocated coming back as per-plan execution progress. Study project practicing DDD and hexagonal architecture within the warehouse-systems fleet.

warehouse-systems · executive briefing · October 2026slotting-optimization
Slot plan generation: pure Planner policy abc-velocity-v1 ranks SKUs by order-line picks (ABC classes at 80%/95% cumulative pick share), filters eligibility by hazmat, temperature and unit capacity, limits churn via stickiness to the previous map, and emits assignments, moves (Assign/Relocate/Vacate) and unassigned SKUs with reasons Human approval workflow: Draft -> Approved/Rejected with immutable terminal states, at most one Approved plan per site (use-case layer plus partial unique index), and automatic supersession of the site's previous Approved plan Case-pick faces (ADR 0012): zones in CASE_PICK_ZONE_CODES become CASE faces with casesCapacity derived from case cube and weight; SKUs planned as pickUnit CASE only when a GCD-of-demand rule holds, backed by a local pack-level copy of product-master events Execution progress tracking (ADR 0010): consumes inventory-storage StockRelocated into a per-plan, per-move progress read model (executed/total, last executed at) exposed on plan reads and MCP/A2A reads; never gates proposals in v1 Read side for operators: GET /forward-slots (the site's CURRENT approved map), GET /sku-velocity (picks and units per SKU over a window, keyset-paginated), full plan reads with assignments, moves, unassigned reasons and execution progress Analytics read side (ADR 0006): slot-plan funnel data product — plans proposed/approved/rejected per day, time-to-approve, supersession rate — projected by a dedicated projector into a separate analytical database and served read-only by cmd/slotting-reports Event-fed local copies (ADR 0003): five always-on consumers over four upstream topics keep demand, product, pack-level, slot and relocation copies convergent in Postgres — no live cross-context calls in either direction

Risks & Watchouts

No auth on REST, MCP or A2A is a deliberate fleet-wide revert; acceptable in a study project but a blocker for any real deployment

Plans are only as good as the event-fed local copies: an empty or stale copy yields an empty or partial plan (mitigated by explicit unassigned reasons instead of guessing)

Slot ranking is the lexical order of location codes — placements can be far from travel-optimal until facility-layout publishes geometry; the deterministic policy hides this behind a port

Demand timing is proxied: due_at is the only date SiteSkuDemandChanged carries (promise cutoff, not pick time)…
